    About the studio

    Ossuary Works

    Small games, made in the dark. Short, punishing fantasy you can play in a browser tab.

    Why we make them

    Ossuary Works started with one question: what if a dark fantasy game fit in a lunch break? No launcher, no install, no forty-hour campaign. Just a tab, a fight, and a reason to come back tomorrow.

    Every game we make is short enough to finish and hard enough to remember. Your progress saves on your own device, so you can close the tab mid-descent and pick up on the same floor.

    Between games we make the things we'd want to wear: heavyweight prints of the knights, saints and ruins from our worlds.

    The Ossuary Works team

    Short

    Each game can be finished in an evening. Runs last minutes, not hours.

    Punishing

    Turn-based, but unforgiving. Every choice costs something, and the floor below remembers.

    In your browser

    Open a link and play, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to download, nothing to update.
